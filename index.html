Our Origin

Fruit, Not Fuss

Pulp & Peel started with a simple question: why does “healthy” have to feel like a punishment?

Our founder, a former line cook, was tired of the guilt, rules, and joyless side of clean eating. So we went back to what makes food fun: real fruit, bold flavors, and no lectures.

Built for busy mornings, post-gym cravings, and quick lunch breaks, Pulp & Peel keeps things fresh, fast, and full of personality.

No guilt. No lectures. Just good fruit.
